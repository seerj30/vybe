# P2: Questions for counsel

Send these questions with the P2 documents ([README](README.md)). Open questions of the signed decisions stay in
[decision 1](../decisions/01-age-policy.md), [decision 8](../decisions/08-legal-wording.md) and the
[legal drafts](../legal/). Tax questions go to an accountant (marked **[tax]**).

Facts for counsel: VYBE is a browser game for adults (18+, self-declared month and year of birth). The operator is
one private person in Romania with no company today. Servers in Frankfurt (Oracle VM, Supabase `eu-central-1`).
No real players yet. Today players write no text that other players see, and nothing moves between players.

## 0. Status of the operator

1. Which duties below apply to a private person, and which only to a company or a registered trader?
2. Does the operator count as a "micro or small enterprise" under the Digital Services Act (Art. 19 exemptions)?
3. Which legal form (PFA, SRL, other) fits a small game with optional paid content? **[tax]** for the tax side.

## 1. Chat ([01-chat](01-chat.md))

1. Is VYBE a hosting service under the DSA today (reports, profiles), with no free text? Which DSA articles apply now?
2. Preset quick-chat (fixed phrases): does it change anything?
3. Friends-only direct messages: is VYBE then an "online platform"? Are private 1:1 messages "dissemination to the public"?
4. Is a DM feature a number-independent interpersonal communications service (ePrivacy, EECC)? May VYBE scan messages with a word filter or a vendor classifier?
5. Public chat in spaces: do terrorist-content removal orders (1-hour removal, Regulation 2021/784) apply? What contact must be reachable, and when?
6. What must a notice-and-action flow and a statement of reasons contain? Is the current report form (reason + 500 chars) enough?
7. Retention of messages and of reported messages; deletion of a sender's messages in the recipient's view on account deletion.
8. Law-enforcement requests: who can ask, what must VYBE keep, and how must VYBE answer?
9. Duty to report suspected offences that threaten life or safety (DSA Art. 18): process for a one-person operator.

## 2. Real-time spaces ([02](02-real-time-spaces.md))

1. Presence (who is online, in which instance, with whom): lawful basis and notice wording.
2. Is in-memory presence with no history enough for data minimization?
3. Does a "private" profile need to hide the player's name in public spaces?

## 3. Trading and gifting ([03](03-trading.md))

1. Terms change from "no transfer" to "gift from the shop to a friend, no resale": wording and consumer-law effects.
2. Does gifting or item-for-item trade give V-Coins or items a monetary value (consumer, tax, anti-money-laundering, payment-services rules)?
3. If paid content exists later, which transfer rules must change?

## 4. Monetization ([04](04-monetization.md))

1. Withdrawal right for a one-time supporter pack: consent and acknowledgment text, durable-medium confirmation, and the online withdrawal function (Directive (EU) 2023/2673).
2. Digital Content Directive: conformity period and end-of-service wording for cosmetic items.
3. Direct EUR prices vs. a paid currency: which rules (EU key principles on in-game virtual currencies, 2024) apply to each?
4. Is the self-declared adults-only gate enough for paid content? What to do with a purchase or chargeback by a minor?
5. Merchant of record vs. own payment processor: who is the seller to the player; what must VYBE's terms still say?
6. Payment provider role under GDPR (processor or independent controller); notice update.
7. Ads (if ever): consent, DSA ad transparency, processors.
8. **[tax]** VAT: OSS registration, the EUR 10,000 EU-wide threshold, Romanian VAT registration, rate.
9. **[tax]** Invoicing: RO e-Factura for B2C sales; does a merchant of record remove this duty?
10. **[tax]** Income tax and social contributions for PFA vs. SRL micro-enterprise at small revenue.

## 5. Countries

1. Which countries can VYBE serve with chat, gifting or paid content? Do any need a local representative?
2. Do any launch countries treat cosmetic gifting or paid cosmetics differently (gambling, consumer law)?
